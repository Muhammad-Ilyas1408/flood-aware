# Executive Summary

Flood-Aware is an AI-assisted flood decision support platform designed to improve situational awareness and decision-making during flood events within the Swat River Basin, Pakistan.

The system integrates real-time flood forecasting, GIS-based spatial analysis, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Large Language Models (LLMs) into a unified decision-support workflow.

Rather than functioning as a simple chatbot, Flood-Aware acts as an intelligent analytical assistant capable of combining multiple sources of evidence before producing grounded recommendations.

The project is being developed as both:

- Final Year Project
- Professional AI Engineering Portfolio Project

Accordingly, the software follows production-oriented engineering practices.

---

# Vision

Develop a reliable AI decision support platform capable of assisting both citizens and disaster management authorities by transforming fragmented flood-related information into actionable intelligence.

---

# Mission

Provide trustworthy, explainable, GIS-aware flood risk assessments through modern AI technologies while maintaining transparency, modularity, and engineering best practices.

---

# Geographic Scope

This system is designed exclusively for:

- Swat River Basin
- Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
- Pakistan

The current architecture intentionally avoids nationwide deployment to maintain implementation focus and regional accuracy.
